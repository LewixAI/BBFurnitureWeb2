Eco Botanic II

Johor Bahru June 2025

A balanced fusion of modern design and timeless materials, offering a living environment that is both elegant and environmentally conscious. Clean lines and contemporary silhouettes are paired with the rich textures and character of reclaimed teak wood, creating a harmonious blend of old and new. This thoughtful combination not only highlights the natural beauty of sustainable materials but also brings warmth, depth, and authenticity to the space. The result is a refined yet inviting atmosphere that celebrates craftsmanship, comfort, and responsible design.
